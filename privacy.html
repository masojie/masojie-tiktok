Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Data we access

When you connect your TikTok account, the App receives an access token and, depending on the scopes you approve, basic profile information (display name, avatar), your video list with public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares), and the ability to upload or publish videos you provide.

2. How we use it

Data is used only to provide the App's features: posting your videos and showing statistics for your own videos. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored securely by the authentication provider used by the App (Composio) and are not published anywhere. Videos you upload are sent to TikTok and are not kept by the App after upload.

4. Retention and deletion

You may revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and services. To request deletion of any stored data, email the address below.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.
